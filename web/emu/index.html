FM1 knobs and buttons

Requires PIGEON firmware with browser panel support. This temporarily stops local playback and routes the FM1 panel to the browser. Disconnect or close this page to return to local control. Select matching FM1 ports; this mode requests MIDI SysEx access.

Make yourself at home.

Click or tap the keys to play chops. Turn ALGORITHM to choose Song 1, drums, or Song 2. SELECT changes tempo. Drag a knob up and down, or scroll over it to turn it. K1 / K2 edit chop start / end; K3 / K4 scroll and zoom.

Press SEQ to open the step grid, REC to record, and PLAY to start or stop. SCL opens patterns and arrangement; ARP opens the mixer. Shift-click holds a button; click it again to release.

Your samples and virtual instrument save in this browser. USB transfers, firmware installation, and physical recording are outside this emulator.

Built on open-source work.

The browser emulator is adapted from Charles Vestal’s FM1 X0X emulator: its simulated FM1 and WebAssembly wrapper, AudioWorklet audio runtime, and interactive browser panel underpin this version.

Felucca’s browser adaptation and firmware are by Leo Kuroshita and Hügelton Instruments. PIGEON is created and directed by The Shrimp Blimp. The emulator runs PIGEON’s firmware against a simulated FM1.
