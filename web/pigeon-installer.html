02Prepare your FM1

Before installing PIGEON from another custom firmware, revert to official M-VAVE V15 using your current firmware’s documented stock-return procedure. Then return here to install PIGEON.

Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Charge the FM1 fully, connect a USB data cable directly to the computer (no hub), keep the computer awake, stop playback, and close other MIDI apps.

Open the sample tool to save a complete recovery archive ↗

Not connected

03Install PIGEON

Keep the FM1 powered and connected until the update finishes and the instrument restarts. This candidate includes a recovery boot stub adapted from ChoralRoot FM-1 by Quixotic7 and its contributors. After an interrupted update, switch on and reconnect to finish. Hold OCT- alone at power-on to request update mode. The first installation of the stub, or a later stub change, has an unprotected window while its first sector is written. Hardware recovery verification is pending.

Complete the checks above to install.

Transfer details


Successful installs send an anonymous completion event to PIGEON’s counter. No sample data or device identity is sent. Offline reports wait until you reconnect.
